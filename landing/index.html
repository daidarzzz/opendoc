§ 0Preface

Free software under GPL‑3.0. Read it, fork it, make it better.

Offline documentation is nothing new. What was missing was a reader that feels as quick as your editor and as calm as a good book. OpenDoc opens in an instant and stays out of the way, so you can get back to the thing you were actually building.

§ 1Search

Try cpp:vector, or rust,go:channel to search two docsets at once.

Results before you finish typing.

Press Ctrl K anywhere and type what you’re looking for. OpenDoc looks through all your docs at once and puts the best match first.

<50ms to search across 300,000 entries1
How was this measured?

1 Matching by nucleo-matcher, the engine behind the Helix editor’s picker. Times depend on your hardware and the size of your library.

2 Zeal’s search rebuilt from its source code (fzy scoring inside SQLite, docsets in parallel) and run beside opendoc-core 0.1.2 on the same machine: Windows, 12 threads, median of three runs. Six queries typed letter by letter over the real CSS, C++, Python 3 and Swift indexes; the larger libraries are copies of them. This times the search engines, not the windows around them. The trade-off: OpenDoc reads every index into memory when it starts, about 0.06 s for these four docsets.

§ 2Reading

Right‑click anywhere for a small, quiet menu instead of the browser’s.

Made for reading, not just looking things up.

Tabs with a memory

Every tab keeps its own back and forward history. Drag to reorder; middle‑click a link to open it right beside the one you’re reading.

Picks up where you left off

Close the app and open it tomorrow: your tabs, history and place on the page are right where you left them.

No white flashes

Pages load before you click, so moving around feels instant — and going back too.

Find, zoom, scroll

Find words on the page, zoom that it remembers, and smooth scrolling that feels at home on any trackpad.

§ 3Library

Already have docsets in a folder? Point OpenDoc at it and you’re done.

A catalog built in, a library in one click.

OpenDoc comes with a catalog of docsets built in. Open it, pick what you need, and it installs with one click. Already have docsets in a folder? Point OpenDoc at it.

And hundreds more in the catalog. Hover over any icon to see its name.

§ 4Internals

External links always open in your own browser, never inside the viewer.

Small, quick, and careful with what it opens.

Docs come from the internet, so every page opens in a sealed‑off frame and links to other sites open in your own browser. The whole app weighs a few megabytes.
